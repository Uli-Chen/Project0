Although job-ready skills are attractive, I believe the main task of a university is to teach students how to think.

Firstly, academic training is very important and good for society. Students who learn how to search for evidence and judge information can move to a new industry without fear.

“Very important” and “good for society”: good in what way? Your outline has a line about changing careers.
